## The rule (C7, first bullet)

**Where it is held.** The drafting rules decide it: Notes carry no obligation (STND.DRAFT.5, STND.DRAFT.12), so the force sits in STND.CLASS.10 and the rule by which a document sets its fields sits in the Notes of STND.CLASS, beside the reading of the table that they already hold. A new provision was weighed and not drafted: the rule is a reading of the column Part of STND.CLASS.10, and a separate provision would instantiate the same twenty-two LVL0 provisions and name each in its text (STND.DRAFT.24) to say what STND.CLASS.10 already says.

**STND.CLASS.10, first sentence**, the clause after the table's own:

> Above the floor, work of CSQ3 and of every more severe class SHALL meet every obligation of VV, REPRO, INTPR, DSGN, SIMPL, RSRC, and QA in full, except each part in the table of this provision, which applies from the class that the table gives, and each part of a provision below LVL0 to which the field `onset` of that provision gives a class, which applies from that class (GOV.11).

The edit replaces the words "which applies from the class that the table gives (GOV.11)." wherever round c leaves them; the second sentence, which round c amends, is untouched. Modals stay two; no identifier is added.

**STND.CLASS Notes, Reading the standard**, a paragraph after "Work of CSQ3 meets the seven proportioned Articles…":

> Below LVL0, the field `onset` of a provision (STND.DRAFT#representation-in-the-harness) states the class from which each of its parts applies, and STND.CLASS.10 gives the field its force. The fields follow one rule: a part applies from the row of the table of STND.CLASS.10 whose named item the part asks for, a reference oracle, a schema, a contract, a documented limit of validity, or another item of the column Part, and otherwise from CSQ3. A part that instantiates the obligation of a row without asking for its item applies from CSQ3, so that a rule of the form of code that rests on DSGN.4 or DSGN.5, and the catch of a failure before it crosses a foreign frame, apply from CSQ3 with the other defaults of form: each costs nothing when the code is written, and a rewrite of each interface when it first applies at a reclassification. Where a field departs from the rule, the Notes of its document give the reason: a less severe class tightens above the floor (PRIME.15), and a more severe class keeps the methods of a standard beyond its core out of work of CSQ3, while the obligation of LVL0 that such a part serves applies at CSQ3 in full. A binding provision, a provision that applies at every class, and a permission carry no field.

This replaces both former rules: the profiles' and NUMER's "applies from the least severe class at which those criteria require an LVL0 obligation it instantiates", and the standards' "The part of a provision that rests on a row … applies from the class of that row". No Notes paragraph of the corpus states either any more.

## The field (C7, second bullet)

**Definition**, in STND.DRAFT#representation-in-the-harness, where every field of a provision is defined, after `checked_by`:

> - `onset`: the class from which the provision applies under STND.CLASS.10, written as one class, `"CSQ3"`, or, where a part starts at a more severe class than the rest, as a list of the class of the provision followed by one pair of a part and its class for each such part, `["CSQ3", ["sentence 2", "CSQ2"]]`. A part is `sentence N`, the Nth sentence of the body outside its tables, counted at terminal punctuation, or `row CELL`, the row of a table of the body whose first cell is CELL. The class of the provision is CSQ0 to CSQ3, or CSQ4 in a list, for a provision that applies at every class except its named parts. A binding provision, a provision of LVL0, a provision that applies whole at every class, and a permission carry no field. The harness fails a malformed value, a field on another item, and a part that the body does not hold.

**Why this is the least that serves the rule.** The rule assigns a class to each part, so the field needs exactly three things, and has nothing else:
- one class for the whole provision, the common case (737 of 766 fields);
- a way to give a later class to a named part, since 29 provisions start in part later than the rest; the part is named by what a reader can point to, a sentence or a table row, which are the only units at which the corpus's Notes have ever stated onsets;
- nothing where the provision binds or applies at every class, so that the binding provisions and the LVL0 provisions are untouched, and the rows of STND.CLASS.10 are not restated in LVL0 (GOV.31).

It has no earlier-part form: a part that starts earlier than the rest is not needed, because the class of the provision is its earliest class; CSQ4 appears only as the class of a list, for STND.CODE.20, whose marker applies wherever a high-assurance coding standard is adopted while its record starts at CSQ1. It has no field for the reason of an onset, which stays in the Notes, read once per document, and no per-clause part, since a clause cannot be pointed to without restating the sentence. A rejected alternative was to leave CSQ3 implicit for every waivable provision, which would have set only about 160 fields; the brief and the ruling ask the onset of each provision to stand in its front matter, so that `show` gives it without a derivation and an omitted field is not read as CSQ3 by accident. The harness checks what it can read: a well-formed value, a field only on a waivable provision below LVL0, and parts that exist; which row a part asks for is a reader's judgment, and the table in `round_o_fields.py` gives the source of each value so that the review can check it.

**The fields set.** 774 rows, one per waivable provision below LVL0: 766 fields and 8 provisions without one (STND.CLASS.10, STND.CLASS.18, STND.CLASS.21, and STND.CLASS.23, which apply at every class; the permissions STND.DEPS.10, STND.DOCS.6, STND.DOCS.7, and STND.WPROC.11, which STND.CLASS.10 does not stage). `apply_fields` refuses a waivable provision without a row, so a provision that round c adds is caught.
