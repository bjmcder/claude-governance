# STND.HARN.28 pair review: STND.LEAN (2026-10-24)

Scope: the 1,046 pairs of `pairs.md` for the draft `draft-0.1.md` (STND.LEAN, the Lean standard, 31 provisions), read against the rehearsed copy `/tmp/rehearsal-d13ud3p5/governance` (STND/LEAN 1.md to 31.md and `_doc.md`). The rehearsed copy was still present. I compared the draft with the 31 provision files by script: every provision body appears verbatim in the draft, every "Instantiates" line equals the `instantiates` field of its file, and the Default or Binding label matches (STND.LEAN.5 is the only binding provision, rank 0, not waivable). The other documents were read from `REPO/harness/governance`; the research note `REPO/research/lean-notes/l1-lean4.md` was read only to spot-check the figures that the Notes quote (1,673, 5,714, 15,693, and 20,458 lines; about 40 percent of statements in error; 20 to 50 percent slower; nine patterns; the 13 to 1 ratio), which it supports.

Findings are ranked Defect, Should-fix, Optional. A Defect is a broken rule or a factual error; the rest are preferences or gaps. Section "Findings" gives each with a concrete fix.

## Method

- Read: STND.HARN.28; the brief (Common and Review agents); the draft in full (Purpose, Terms, Notes, 31 provisions, References); the bodies of the 95 distinct other provisions in the pair list (AGENT.3, 4, 5, 12, 22, 41, 47; CPS.3 to CPS.9; HDES.3 to HDES.15 with HDES.ASIC and HDES.FPGA; HW.3 to HW.20 and the sub-profile provisions HW.ASIC, HW.CPU, HW.EMBED, HW.FPGA, HW.QPU; MEAS.4 to MEAS.7; ML.3 to ML.7; NUMER.3 to NUMER.29); and, for the hand checks, VV.1 to VV.10, DSGN.1 to DSGN.17, PRIME.2, PRIME.4, PRIME.13, GOV.50, QA.7, QA.9, QA.10, INTPR.1, INTPR.10, SIMPL.6, REPRO.1, REPRO.7, the Lexicon entry Axiom, STND.CLASS.10 and STND.CLASS.18, STND.CONC (Notes and CONC.28), STND.VVUQ (1 to 3, 13, 17, 18, 36 to 38, 51, 62, 64, 78, 82, 83), STND.CODE in full, STND.DEPS in full, AGENT (Terms, AGENT.2 to 5, 10 to 12, 47), NUMER (the provisions on floating point and tolerance), and in STND.CXX, STND.PY, STND.JL the naming, failure-response, assertion, deprecation, and assumed-condition provisions (CXX.14, CXX.16 to CXX.18; PY.1, PY.22, PY.25 to PY.30; JL.6, JL.13 to JL.15, JL.18, JL.35).
- Shared-matters tables read (provision 2 of each profile): HW.2, NUMER.2, CPS.2, HDES.2, HDES.ASIC.2, HDES.FPGA.2, HW.ASIC.2, HW.CPU.2, HW.EMBED.2, HW.FPGA.2, HW.GPU.2, HW.QPU.2, MEAS.2, ML.2 (AGENT.2 holds no table). No row of any table lists a STND.LEAN provision, and none lists a matter of proof, a proof assistant, or a model checker. The rows that touch Lean are the hardware contract and delivered arithmetic (HW.2 row 1; HW.5), the agreement of alternative execution paths (HW.2 row 3; HW.15 to HW.19), precision a result requires and approximation disclosure (NUMER.2 rows 1 and 4; NUMER.5, 12, 16, 17), the tolerance basis (NUMER.2 row 5; NUMER.11), and the design record of a realized design, where HDES.6 to HDES.15 allow proof as evidence (HDES.2 row 1).
- Completeness of the list: I recomputed the pair set from the front matter (each STND.LEAN provision against each provision of AGENT, CPS, HDES*, HW*, ML, MEAS, NUMER that instantiates one of the same DSGN, SIMPL, REPRO, VV, RSRC, INTPR, PRIME, PRIN, or QA provisions). It gives the same 1,046 pairs, with the same bases; none missing, none extra. Two STND.LEAN provisions have no listed pair because no profile provision shares their bases: STND.LEAN.12 (SIMPL.6) and STND.LEAN.30 (DSGN.7). STND.LEAN.11 has one pair (ML.5), STND.LEAN.27 one (NUMER.4), and STND.LEAN.5 five (PRIME.2). The provisions without a pair are checked by hand below.
- Grouping: most pairs are of two kinds. The first is a Lean provision against a profile provision that shares only VV.1, INTPR.10, VV.5, VV.6, VV.8, VV.3, REPRO.1, REPRO.2, REPRO.7, or DSGN.2 with it, on an unrelated or distantly related subject (for instance STND.LEAN.15 against HDES.ASIC.9). The second is where a Lean proof can serve as the evidence that the profile provision asks (HDES.6 to HDES.15, CPS.4 to CPS.8, HW.15 to HW.17, NUMER.5 to NUMER.12). Pairs of the first kind are in group rows with one reason per group of other provisions; pairs of the second kind have rows of their own with the Lean provisions that meet them. Rows group Lean provisions only where the reason holds for each, and the reason says what differs. Every pair is in exactly one row, checked by script (see "Coverage check").
- Tests applied. Conflict: can one be met only by failing the other, or do they answer one question differently as written. Unowned: do they address one matter that no shared-matters table gives to an owner. A matter whose only profile provisions are in one profile, and where the other side is an LVL2 standard or an LVL0 provision, is not counted as unowned unless the standard adds a matter that the profile does not hold (the precedent of the review of STND.OFFLD, ledger 115).
- STND.LEAN.5 is binding. I tested it as the brief asks: against PRIME.2 word by word (does it ask more than PRIME.2 forbids), against GOV.11 (never raise the floor), and against each listed and hand-checked provision that it could meet, in particular AGENT.3, AGENT.4, AGENT.47, STND.LEAN.15, STND.LEAN.1, and STND.DEPS.6. Every other STND.LEAN provision is a default (SHOULD or MAY); where a binding provision of the other side applies, a conflict is recorded only if the default cannot be met together with it.

- Coverage check: a script reads `pairs.md`, assigns each pair to exactly one row of the table below by the pair of (STND.LEAN provision, other provision), and fails on a pair in no row or in two rows. Result: 1,046 pairs, numbers 1 to 1046, each in one row; no row empty. The row tables in this file were generated from the same rows, and the pair numbers in the first column were parsed back from this file and compared with the list (section "Coverage check").

## Findings

### Defect

1. **STND.LEAN.5, sentence 2: 'built each declaration' asks more than PRIME.2 (binding provision; GOV.11).** The binding text allows a report 'proved' only on a check 'that built each declaration on which the theorem depends'. A project that imports a library shipped with the toolchain or fetched as a build product never builds those declarations; a checker (or the maker of the toolchain) accepted them. On a literal reading no such work can report a theorem as proved at any class, and the argument in the Notes that LEAN.5 asks nothing that PRIME.2 does not already forbid fails: PRIME.2 forbids claiming a check that was not run, not reporting on a check that relied on checked build products. LEAN.15 (the replay covers fetched build products) and DEPS.6 (digest of every fetched component) already hold that case as defaults. The Terms of the draft show the same split: a Lean package is fetched and built, while the toolchain is installed. Fix: 'A theorem SHALL be reported as proved only on a check, at the reported revision of its source, that checked each declaration of the work on which the theorem depends (PRIME.2).' and add a sentence to the Notes that declarations of the toolchain and of fetched build products stay with STND.LEAN.15 and STND.DEPS.6. Sentence 1 is sound.

### Should-fix

1. **Native evaluation against the accepted axiom list (LEAN.14, LEAN.6, LEAN.7; VV.9, GOV.50).** LEAN.14 lets a relied-on theorem depend on native evaluation with a record; LEAN.6 fails the check for each axiom declaration outside the accepted axiom list; LEAN.7 limits that list to logical axiom declarations and axioms the human owner declared, and the Terms exclude native evaluation from 'logical axiom declaration'. So LEAN.14 can be used only when the human owner has declared the native-evaluation axiom, which the draft never says; as written the three cannot be met together without that declaration. Fix: in LEAN.14 or the Notes, say that the theorem passes LEAN.6 only where the accepted list holds the declaration, and that the list may hold it only under LEAN.7; or, if the custodian reads the compiler's trust as an assumption of the evidence like kernel soundness, add 'and the axiom declaration of native evaluation that a record under STND.LEAN.14 covers' to LEAN.7 and record it as a custodian decision, because it widens what the Lexicon calls an Axiom. Related: the draft treats the logical axiom declarations as assumptions of the evidence and not as Axioms of the Lexicon; that reading is the draft's own and should go to the custodian with the Notes' argument stated as a decision.
2. **LEAN.4, row Statement review: no outcome (VV.5).** Add the outcome and the not-applicable case: 'The review of STND.LEAN.11, with its reviewer, its date, and whether it found that the statement says what the claim needs; or that validation does not apply for want of a real-world referent'. Then the Notes' sentence that the record 'keeps verification and validation apart, as VV.5 asks' holds.
3. **LEAN.25: sentence 1 against sentence 2, and the list of responses (PRIME.4, siblings).** Sentence 1 has code 'not respond by a panic' to a PRIME.4 condition, and sentence 2 accepts a run that ends at its first panic, which the Notes say a runtime setting can give. Under sentence 1 a panic that halts is still a panic. Fix: sentence 1 'and not respond by a panic that lets the run continue', or define the halting panic as one of the listed halts. Align the list with CXX.16 and JL.15: add 'enter the designed safe state' and 'degrade visibly with a diagnosis' or cite the response list of PRIME.4 and say that these are the admitted forms; the provision instantiates DSGN.2 and SIMPL.6, and the siblings instantiate PRIME.4 (the rationale for instantiating DSGN.2 only is the custodian's open question that the Notes state).
4. **Terms of the draft.** The Lean row says 'in its fourth major form'; the custodian's standing direction is no version numbers (GOV.18); delete the phrase. The opening line of the Terms imports Execution setting from STND.CODE, and no provision or Note uses the term; delete the import or use the term in LEAN.12 and LEAN.28 where a setting is meant.
5. **LEAN.27: no allowance for names fixed by the documented notation.** CXX.14, PY.1, and JL.6 each add that a name fixed by the notation documented under NUMER.4 MAY keep its form (INTPR.4, STND.DOCS.12). NUMER.4 (binding) has names map to the notation in the code. Add the sentence, or say in the Notes that the convention named in the repository configuration carries it.

2. **LEAN.22:** add 'The review of the other parts of the change is unchanged (QA.7)'.

4. **Notes on LEAN.16 and the CSQ0 row:** say that the CSQ0 row of STND.CLASS.10 is met equally by exhaustive checking outside Lean, so that the sentence on native evaluation limits only the Lean route.
5. **LEAN.31 and LEAN.4:** (a) LEAN.31 differs from JL.35 in scope (build, not test run) and in what may be listed; say that the listing exempts a warning raised inside a dependency, since a work cannot fix those. (b) LEAN.4, Assumptions row: add 'and where each is shown to hold' so that the row meets VV.1's 'shown to be met'.
6. **Memory model and concurrent code:** add to the row Code that runs of LEAN.4 (or to LEAN.19) 'and, for concurrent code, the memory and ordering model of the model (HW.11)'; the Notes now carry it as something that the Notes of STND.CONC 'still ask'.
