# Cold review of STND.FTN draft 0.1

## Verified by script
- `govharness verify` on the rehearsal passes all eight checks; one g.18 warning prints, but `findings.py 'STND\.FTN'` is empty and my count gives at most two modals per provision, one per sentence.
- All 40 provisions: titles at most six words; body IDs equal instantiates plus refers_to; no excluded expression of STND.DRAFT.7; no compiler, flag, or version in a provision; FTN.31 to .33 binding, 37 defaults. Sentences citing NUMER.5, HW.3, or HW.5 also cite a LVL0 provision.
- STND.MPI has no match for Fortran, ASYNCHRONOUS, or subarray. Notes mention FTN.27 and FTN.28 zero times.
- By hand only: overlaps, onsets, f1 accuracy. I did not read the MPI, OpenMP, or Fortran standards.

## Defects
1. **FTN.35 sentence 2 blocks its own adoption.** "A change SHOULD NOT add an entry" excepts only external code, yet adoption must enter every existing file; STND.PY.6, the stated model, puts "the change that adopts" in the provision, while here it is only in an unnumbered closing paragraph. A new processor, a later revision, or a new provision causes the same trap. Fix: add "the change that adopts this standard, or a change of a processor or of the revision in force" to the exception; delete the closing paragraph.
2. **FTN.33 sentence 2 is binding on an unverified hazard, and the Notes call it settled.** The ruling of 2026-10-23 covers STND.CXX.18 only, yet the Notes state "None asks for work that PRIME.4 does not (GOV.11)" as fact, not as a decision for the custodian. Barring the option is within PRIME.4 only through STND.CODE.6 (an option that deletes a required check is an execution setting that removes it), and that holds only if the hazard is real; f1 shows none for Fortran. A binding sentence may rest on a hazard only if its truth does not depend on it. Fix: word it by documentation, "an option that, by the documentation of the processor, lets it assume ...", and list it as a numbered decision for the custodian; failing that, make it a default.
3. **Notes carry obligations in the indicative (STND.DOCS).** Floating-point paragraph: "Code that detects these exceptions without halting reads the flags ... before it relies on their results" (no provision holds it) and "a negative answer is a precondition violation, answered as STND.FTN.31 requires" (FTN.19 does not say so). Legacy paragraph: "an agent does not change the work only to remove it". Revisions paragraph: "a project at an earlier revision avoids a form already marked". Fix: move the first two into FTN.18 and FTN.19, or recast as hazards; drop the others.

## Should-fix
4. **FTN.32 is hard to parse.** "a statement whose STAT=, IOSTAT=, or CMDSTAT= specifier, or of a call of an intrinsic procedure whose status argument, receives a value". Fix: "Where a STAT=, IOSTAT=, or CMDSTAT= specifier, or the status argument of an intrinsic procedure, receives a value that reports a condition that PRIME.4 lists, code SHALL respond as STND.FTN.31 requires before it relies on the outcome of that statement or call."
5. **FTN.33 sentence 1 versus CODE.6.** CODE.6 asks the check "where untrusted data enters"; FTN.33 asks a bounds test "before its use" at each subscript from untrusted data, which can be later and more numerous. DSGN.2 and PRIME.4 support it (an out-of-range subscript is silent continuation), but the Notes say the reach "is that of STND.CODE.6, which this standard does not widen", which is not quite true. Fix: state the cost (one test per subscript) and why the entry check does not suffice (no language check exists), or tie it to "a value for which STND.CODE.6 requires a check". The Notes say it does not widen CODE.6's reach, which is not quite true. Fix: say why the entry check does not suffice (no language check exists), or tie the sentence to a value for which CODE.6 requires a check.
6. **FTN.4 belongs in STND.BUILD.** BUILD.1 and .2 declare inputs, including those a tool writes at each run; BUILD.3 requires an incremental build to equal a clean one; the BUILD cold review (item 7) already asks for Fortran module files. FTN.4 is those outcomes in Fortran words, with weak ancestors (DSGN.7, VV.8). Fix: delete it; add a Notes sentence that module files are generated inputs under BUILD.2 to .4; ask BUILD to name module and submodule files.
7. **FTN.38 sentence 2 is STND.BUILD.12** (and CODE.4's suppress-with-reason). Delete it. Sentence 1 repeats FTN.5 and FTN.6 as a check; fold it into them.
8. **FTN.2 sentence 1 restates BUILD.11 and HW.5.** Only options that change default kinds or the initial value of locals are new; cut to those with a cite.
9. **FTN.16 sentence 1 (a kind on every real literal) is disproportionate.** The hazard (f1 item 7) is a literal that meets an entity of another kind. With FTN.35, nearly every legacy file is listed for this alone. Fix: "Each real literal constant that is assigned to, combined with, or passed to a real or complex entity of a kind other than default real SHOULD carry that kind parameter."
10. **FTN.35 as a whole.** The nine named provisions are an arbitrary subset (not FTN.3, 9, 11, 13, 18 to 21, 38), and the Term "Legacy list" says "each source file ... that does not meet this standard", wider than FTN.35. A reason per form per file is a heavy inventory. Fix: align the Term; allow one reason per class of forms; say why the rest are not listed. "Only shrinks" is proportionate once item 1 is fixed.
11. **FTN.22 and its Notes disagree; f1 contradicts itself.** The provision speaks of clauses; the Notes cite "a threadprivate common block is named as a block", and a threadprivate directive is not a clause. f1 item 14 ("as a whole or by member") contradicts its table row. Fix: "each directive or clause of OpenMP that privatizes members ... SHOULD name the whole block", mark the clause rule unverified, and have f1 item 14 corrected.
12. **MPI scope beyond f1.** f1 supports ASYNCHRONOUS and the register routine for nonblocking buffers only (S4 20.1.17.1); FTN.24 adds persistent, one-sided, and split collective operations. f1 item 16 covers one-sided and split-collective for subarrays but not persistent. FTN.25 asks "contiguous", but the MPI condition f1 quotes is "subscript triplets", and a unit-stride section is still a triplet. Fix: check S4 20.1.12 and 20.1.17 and extend f1, or restrict to what f1 shows. I could not check the MPI text; also compare FTN.23's "states as consistent with the Fortran standard" with f1's "fully supports".
13. **No Notes for FTN.27 and FTN.28**: add the NEWUNIT rule and the order of parallel output records (f1 item 18); STND.CONC does not hold output order.
14. **FTN.1 sentence 2 overreaches**: "each form that the revision in force does not define" is wider than a processor must report (the Notes say "review decides the rest"). Fix: "each form that the numbered syntax rules and constraints do not permit, and each obsolescent form". The Term "Revision in force" is circular with FTN.1; define it as the revision the configuration assigns to the file.

## Optional
15. FTN.36 restates VV.3 and VV.8; fold it into FTN.37, whose table is new.
16. "The list grows with each revision" overstates f1 (2023 added nothing). Term "Processor" departs from the Fortran standard's meaning; consider "Toolchain". FTN.18 does not say which value the modes take.
17. Proportion: 40 provisions and 4,400 words of Notes against 31 to 35 provisions in CXX, PY, JL, OFFLD (OFFLD Notes 2,700). Items 6, 7, 15 remove two provisions and a sentence; trimming the study-extract sentences (S5 to S8) saves about 400 words.

## Binding provisions (GOV.11, GOV.19)
FTN.31 has the CXX.16 form and is sound. FTN.32 asks what PRIME.4 and CODE.7 require, at one test per status, and is sound after item 4. FTN.33: items 2 and 5.

## Checked against f1 and the other standards
- Notes match f1; the points f1 marks unverified (processor options, interface bodies, error termination without STAT=, exit status, integer overflow, mode restoration, SUM order, column 72, BLOCK) are marked in the Notes and none is a provision's premise. FTN.6 holds whichever way host association works.
- MPI .23 to .26 add Fortran content and do not restate MPI.2, 14, or 16 (partial overlaps: FTN.23 s1 with FTN.5; FTN.26 with MPI.14). ASYNCHRONOUS and the register routine read correctly against f1.
- Class onset: the DSGN.4 rows from CSQ2 match the table; the DSGN.15 and DSGN.2 carve-outs (FTN.13, 21 s2, 17) are argued, as in CXX.26. Removing FTN.4 and FTN.38 s2 needs matching edits in the lists.

## Overlaps
FTN.4 with BUILD.1 to .4; FTN.38 s2 with BUILD.12, CODE.4; FTN.2 s1 with BUILD.11, HW.5; FTN.36 with VV.3, VV.8; FTN.33 s1 with CODE.6, DSGN.2; FTN.32 with CODE.7; FTN.17 with CODE.6; FTN.5, 6, 38 with each other.

## Verdict
Revise before adoption. Defects 1 to 3 break rules; the content is otherwise sound and well sourced. The FTN.33 sentence 2 decision goes to the custodian.
