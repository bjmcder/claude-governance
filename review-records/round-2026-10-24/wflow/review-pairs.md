## Observations (not conflicts, not unowned)

Ranked Defect (a broken rule or a factual error), Should-fix, Optional. Each has a fix.

### Defect

1. Factual error in the Notes (paragraph on partial outputs): the removal of a partial output by an agent is said to be "exempt under STND.DFLT.15 only where the item was recorded as temporary from its creation". DFLT.15 has five exempt classes: a file committed and verified recoverable, an item held by a snapshot or backup, content regenerable from recorded inputs (REPRO.1), a temporary item, and an item a checkpoint covers. A partial output of a reproducible step is regenerable content and is exempt under the third class, as GUARD.7 also says. Fix: "exempt under STND.DFLT.15 where it is a temporary item or belongs to another class of that provision, for example content regenerable from recorded inputs, and GUARD.6 prevails over every exemption for a removal whose scope is not enumerated and verified first".
2. Obligations stated in the indicative in the Notes (brief, STND.DOCS): "A write to a remote system by an agent proceeds only under an authorization (PRIME.7), and a retry repeats it within the scope of that authorization" (paragraph on effects) and "so the agent enumerates and verifies the scope before it removes anything" (paragraph on storage). Fix: cite without stating the duty, for example "PRIME.7 governs a write to a remote system by an agent, and a retry is within the scope of its authorization" and "GUARD.6 and STND.DFLT.24 govern the enumeration of scope before a removal".

### Should-fix

3. STND.WFLOW.25 omits the computational environment from the identity of a generation of restart state (inputs, parameters, code revision, number of processes). REPRO.1 lists the environment among recorded inputs; WFLOW.6 and WFLOW.37 include it; WFLOW.26 can refuse a restart only for an item that WFLOW.25 recorded. A restart on another hardware model, library, or arithmetic setting continues another computation (HW.5 sentence 3 asks whether delivered arithmetic is the same on each model; HW.7 confirms the contract at resumption). Fix: add "the identity of its computational environment" to the list of WFLOW.25.
4. STND.WFLOW.4 scopes the key of an effect to "that step in that workflow run", and the Terms make a resumption the start of a new workflow run; an attempt includes one that a resumption runs again, and WFLOW.5 asks that each later attempt give the effects of a single attempt. A key unique to the run is new at each resumption, so a remote write made before a halt is made again. Fix: "under a key that identifies that step and its inputs, is the same for each attempt of the step, and that the receiving system checks".
5. STND.WFLOW.38 (a) leaves out the jobs that the halted run submitted and that are not known to have ended (STND.CONC.29 asks the record of a run to state them); a resumption that starts a step while its earlier attempt still runs gives one final name two writers, and WFLOW.10 and WFLOW.7 do not cover two live attempts; (b) does not say when the record is written. WFLOW.20 keeps the records of completed steps, cache, and index valid after a forced end; the failed steps, attempts, and effects of WFLOW.38 are not in that list, and AGENT.25 has an agent write its record before its next action. Fix: add "each job submitted and not known to have ended" to the list, and "kept current as steps end and valid after a forced end" or extend WFLOW.20 to the record of WFLOW.38.
6. STND.WFLOW.18 and a job of several processes. WFLOW.17 states which processes receive a notice of end; WFLOW.18 has "that process" end at its next safe point. A notice is not a condition that PRIME.4 lists, so STND.CONC.2 and STND.MPI.6 do not require the other processes to halt or be told, and the writes of WFLOW.23 need every process. Fix: add to WFLOW.18 that a process that receives a notice tells the other processes of its job that take part in the write of restart state, with a pointer to STND.MPI for message passing; or state in the Notes that a job of several processes is outside the default.
7. Two internal tensions with the staleness rule. (a) WFLOW.29 sentence 2 reruns "only the parts that are not completed"; a part completed under code or inputs that have since changed is not current under WFLOW.6, and RSRC.7 admits reuse only where it preserves correctness. WFLOW.44 uses "not current". Fix: "not completed or not current under the rule of STND.WFLOW.6". (b) WFLOW.2 sentence 2 reruns each dependent of a varying step that runs again; WFLOW.6 sentence 1 lets the description say that an output produced again unchanged leaves its consumers current (early cutoff). If the rerun output is unchanged the consumers need not rerun. Fix: "each step that depends on an output of that step that differs from the stored output".
8. STND.WFLOW.42 ("compare its outputs and effects") and STND.WFLOW.44 ("results agree") state no agreement, while WFLOW.43 takes the tier from STND.VVUQ.1.
