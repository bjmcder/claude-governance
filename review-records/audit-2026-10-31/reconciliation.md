## Findings

### X1 (High) STND.DFLT.15, sixth exempt class: outside the grounds of GUARD.7

- Location: `STND/DFLT/15.md`, last bullet; Notes of `STND/DFLT/_doc.md`, "The sixth class …".
- Quote: "change or removal of an item, other than a revision of the version history, that the agent created in the current unit of work and that no other actor has changed since its creation." Notes: "its state before the unit is its absence, which its removal restores, and an edit of it overwrites only the agent's own work of that unit … and GUARD.9 asks no confirmation of a prior state for it (GUARD.7)."
- Problem: GUARD.7 lets a register exempt a class only "because its prior state is verifiably recoverable, its content is regenerable from recorded inputs (REPRO.1), or it removes or overwrites only temporary items". The sixth class is none of the three. An agent-created item is not a temporary item (LEX.temporary-item: "recorded as temporary from its creation, that no record cites"). Its prior content is not recoverable after an edit or removal, and "absence restored by removal" does not restore the overwritten content. So the class exempts from GUARD.5, and from the GUARD.9 check, an overwrite or removal without a verified recoverable prior state. That is an irrecoverable action, reserved by PRIME.6. A lower provision that relaxes it is void under PRIME.15. The condition "no other actor has changed it" is also one the agent cannot verify from the record, against GUARD.2. The reach is wider than the stated aim: it covers a long-running result file the agent created, not only a draft.
- Fix (choose one, at ratification): (a) Recommended. Narrow the bullet to what GUARD.7 already admits: "change or removal of an item, other than a revision of the version history, that the agent created in the current unit of work and whose content it regenerates from recorded inputs (REPRO.1), or that is a temporary item". That is redundant with classes 3 and 4, so then drop the class and keep classes 1 and 3 for successive edits. (b) Amend GUARD.7 (LVL0, custodians) with a fourth ground: "or it holds only an item that the agent created in the current unit of work, uncommitted, and that no other actor has changed", and say in DFLT how the agent checks "no other actor" (for example, an unchanged digest since its last write). (b) belongs to the LVL0 transaction, which is already entered, so it cannot ride in this round without re-drafting entry 325.

### X2 (Medium, safety net) Foreign entries taken in on the owner's direction to merge

- Location: term Foreign entry, STND.DFLT Terms ("other than on an act of the human owner given it directly"); Notes of STND.DFLT: "Where the human owner directs an agent to bring in revisions, the direction takes in their entries as the owner's own provisioning of the work".
- Problem: R4 exists to close the route S4 names, a collaborator's `authorize` line arriving by merge. A plain owner direction ("merge PR 12", "pull upstream") takes the entries in as accepted without the owner having seen any ledger line. The attack is: a branch carries an `authorize`, `classify`, or isolation line (STND.DFLT.37) that names the owner as the actor of the act. The owner merges for the code. The agent then relies on the line under PRIME.7 as a grant, and as isolation under PRIME.9. `process/decisions.md` item 2 records this as a deliberate reading and recommends it, but the effect is that the confirmation requirement binds only on merges the agent makes unprompted, which is the rarer case.
- Fix wording: in the term, "other than on an act of the human owner given it directly that names the entries or the ledger lines it brings in"; and in the Notes replace the quoted sentence with "A direction to bring in revisions does not confirm the entries they add to the ledger; the agent lists them in its statement, and STND.DFLT.23 accepts each only when the owner's confirmation names it." If the custodian keeps the present reading, the residual belongs in the STND.DFLT.18 row ("The work, its records, and its history", source "Every actor") as a stated gap (GUARD.16).

### X3 (Medium) GOV.50 and GOV.22 still treat every estimate for implicit expenditure as the owner's

- Location: `GOV/50.md` ("estimates for implicit expenditure (PRIME.8)" among judgments "adopted only by the human owner"); `GOV/22.md` ("No default … authorizes an estimate; only the human owner does."); `docs/adoption.md` repeats GOV.50's list.
- Problem: under PRIME.8 as amended, the estimate that bounds an agent's own operating cost where no limit or fixed cost exists is stated by the agent and authorized by the direction. GOV.50 says the owner alone adopts estimates for implicit expenditure. GOV.22 and the DFLT purpose text ("authorizes an estimate (GOV.22)") are true for the estimate of the first sentence of PRIME.8 and ambiguous for the stated one. `process/decisions.md` relies on GOV.22 ("no default authorizes an estimate") to keep DFLT.33 from setting one, which is right, but GOV.50 contradicts PRIME.8 on its face.
- Fix: GOV.50 and the adoption guide: "estimates for implicit expenditure other than an agent's own operating cost (PRIME.8)". GOV.50 is LVL0, so it takes the next LVL0 amendment. Until then, `lvl0/decisions.md` should record that LVL0 governs it (GOV.1, GOV.42 transition already says so).

### X4 (Medium, put to the custodian) The agent sets its own cap where only the stated estimate bounds the cost

- Location: PRIME.8 sentence 4; AGENT.6; STND.DFLT.33.
- Quote: "the direction authorizes it only where the cost is measurable and only up to a rigorously and faithfully derived estimate that the agent states before the work begins."
- Problem: where the cost is measurable but no spending limit exists and the provisioning fixes no cost (pay-as-you-go with a meter and no cap), the agent authorizes its own spending up to a figure it derives. Nothing in the text gives the owner sight of the figure before the spending. AGENT.6 records it in the plan. STND.DFLT.33 enters the direction and "which bound of PRIME.8 limits that cost", but not the figure. PRIME.7 says no authorization is "granted … by an agent for itself"; PRIME.8 says the grant is the direction's, so the text is defensible, but a direction of a few words ("fix this bug") authorizes a spend that the owner never bounded. The amount is bounded in kind, not in order of magnitude.
- Fix wording (STND.DFLT.33, appended): "… and, where the bound is an estimate that the agent states, the figure of that estimate and the record of the plan that holds it." And in the Notes: "the agent shows that figure in its first message after the direction." Alternative for the custodian: a ceiling the owner records once (a discipline under GUARD.14), without which an agent refers before a stated estimate above a stated size. The lead should list this among the residual risks at ratification.
- Related ambiguity: PRIME.8 lists "an authorized estimate" among the halting bounds but, after its first sentence, an owner-authorized per-task estimate for the agent's own cost (GUARD.14's "a per-task estimate … included") has no route to be the bound: sentence 4 sends a cost with no "spending limit that the human owner sets" to the agent's estimate. Say in the AGENT Notes or in the DFLT Notes that a per-task estimate that the owner sets under GUARD.14 is a spending limit that the owner sets for PRIME.8.

### X8 (Low-Medium) STND.WPROC term Main line contradicts itself where the default branch is the release branch

- Location: `STND/WPROC/_doc.md` Terms: "Main line: Each branch from which the work is released or deployed; … and nor is the branch that the version history designates as its default until a record of the human owner names it as a main line."
- Problem: in the common case the work is released from the default branch. The first limb makes it a main line ("each branch from which … released"), the second says it is not until recorded. An agent merging into it then makes, without a decision, a revision available beyond development, which LEX.release and QA.10 reserve to the owner. The rationale states the intent ("a branch from which the work is in fact released, other than the default branch, stays a main line") but the default branch is the case the term decides the wrong way once a release has happened. WPROC.1, WPROC.11, STND.TEST.5 and STND.TEST.6 (advance of a main line, full-suite run) all key on the term.
- Fix wording: "… and nor is the default branch of the version history, from which the work has not been released or deployed, until a record of the human owner names it as a main line." The Notes then say that once the work is released from it, it is a main line without a record.

### 4. Safety net: PRIME and GUARD as amended, end to end

Read in full. Each prohibited condition was tried against the combined changes: CSQ4 presumption, QA.11 promotion by direction, the GOV.20 narrowing, DFLT.23 acceptance, DFLT.33 entry, DFLT.36 presumption of the owner, DFLT.37 isolation record, PRIME.8 estimate, PRIME.13 precedence, GUARD.1 free tier, GUARD.9, DFLT.15.

Reachable by a compliant agent:
1. An irrecoverable overwrite or removal, of the agent's own new item, without a recoverable prior state (PRIME.6, GUARD.5): through DFLT.15's sixth class (X1). Low harm, but a structural breach.
