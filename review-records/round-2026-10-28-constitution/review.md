## Defects

1. **The LVL0 rationale attributes to the custodian a direction the quoted ruling does not contain.** In `lvl0_rationale`: "a ruling under GOV.3 that reads GOV.20 so without a text change (rejected: the custodian directed an amendment, and under GOV.9 ...)". The custodian's words are "i agree with the recommendation on the open items. proceed", and the recommendation quoted in `decisions.md`, "have GOV.20 apply only to promotions beyond the one QA.11 makes at classification", says how GOV.20 is to apply, not by what instrument; the item of `OPEN-ITEMS.md` that the rationale names as the proposal offers both an amendment and a `rule` under GOV.3. The standing process forbids writing that the custodian has ruled what the custodian has not ruled. Fix: in the script, "(rejected: under GOV.9 the more restrictive reading of the text would keep governing every reader who has not seen the ruling)", and the same in `decisions.md`, "The ruling", first sentence of the second paragraph: "The ruling is carried as an amendment of GOV.20, not as a `rule` under GOV.3, so that the text itself restores ..." stays, and the bullet "A `rule` under GOV.3. The custodian directed an amendment, and under GOV.9 ..." becomes "A `rule` under GOV.3. Under GOV.9 ...". If the recommendation as put to the custodian did name an amendment, quote it whole in `decisions.md`, "The ruling", with where it was made, and the sentence may stand.
2. **The floor argument of the LVL0 rationale misstates the reach.** "the change narrows a LVL0 obligation back to its reach at ledger entry 202 ... as at entry 202". At entry 202, a project of CSQ3 whose immediate team relied on a result needed a promotion by recorded act, and so a constitution; after this change it needs neither. The reach is narrower than at entry 202 for that case, which `decisions.md` ("Floor argument") says plainly and the ledger entry, the permanent record, does not. Fix, in `lvl0_rationale`: "Floor (GOV.11): the change narrows a LVL0 obligation of the floor, on the custodian's ruling under GOV.40, which GOV.11 does not bar, since GOV.11 bars lower governance from raising the floor; a project that promotes a result by its human owner's recorded act, releases software, or drives physical hardware has a project constitution, as at ledger entry 202, and a project of CSQ3 or CSQ4 whose results are relied upon only as QA.11 admits has none, where at entry 202 that reliance needed a promotion by recorded act and so a constitution; the reliance that the classification admits is ..." (the rest unchanged). The revision row needs no change.
