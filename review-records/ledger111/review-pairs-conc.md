## Method

- Read STND.HARN.28, the 28 draft provisions, `_doc.md`, the Notes, and the bodies of the 93 distinct other provisions in the list (AGENT, CPS, HDES and its ASIC/FPGA sub-profiles, HW, HW.CPU/EMBED/GPU/QPU/ASIC/FPGA, MEAS, ML, NUMER), plus PRIME.4 and DSGN.1/3/5/9/14/15/17.
- Shared-matters tables: HW.2, NUMER.2, CPS.2, HDES.2, ML.2, HW.CPU.2, HW.GPU.2, HW.EMBED.2 (others have no row for a listed provision). Matters with no row for the provision in question were treated as unowned candidates only if two or more provisions address them.
- Pairs were grouped by the other provision's family. A group is judged on one reason (stated in its row) and lists its pair numbers. Every pair on the same subject as a CONC provision (failures and halts, asynchronous completion, shared state, run-to-run variation and counts, random streams, run-time checks, real-time bounds, oversubscription, tests across counts) has its own row or sits in a row whose reason was read for that pair. The table lists each pair exactly once (checked by script: 354 pairs, numbers 1-354, no gaps or repeats).
- Conflict test: can one be met only by failing the other, or do they answer one question differently. Unowned test: do they address one matter that no shared-matters table gives.
- Also checked by hand, since the list holds no pair with STND.MPI, CXX, JL, PY, VVUQ, or (for the profile side) HW.CPU.4 and CONC.17/18: STND.MPI.5, .6, .12, .18, .19, .24, .25; STND.CXX.15, .21, .22, .23, .26; STND.JL.5, .24, .27; STND.PY.17; STND.VVUQ.1, .2, .62, .82, .83; HW.CPU.3, .4, .5; AGENT.40. ## Observations (not conflicts, not unowned)

1. CONC.14 asks for a stated behavior for work beyond the bound but not that the behavior be visible; dropping work silently would breach PRIME.4 (binding), which still applies. Optional Note sentence.
2. CONC.21 states results that change with the number of tasks; HW.12 and REPRO.3 speak of identical recorded inputs. The Notes already say CONC.21 adds this; same as MPI review item 8. Optional: say that the declaration also covers runs that differ only in recorded task counts.

## Summary for the adopter

Conflicting pairs:
1. #48, STND.CONC.2 sentence 1 vs HW.8 (PRIME.4): CONC.2 lets a task answer a PRIME.4 failure only by reaching its parent or ending the run. HW.8 and PRIME.4 also allow degrading visibly or a designed safe state, and a task with no parent (interrupt or signal handler) cannot do either. Suggested fix: "SHALL reach the parent, or be answered by that task as PRIME.4 requires".
2. #339, STND.CONC.4 sentence 2 vs HW.11: CONC.4 asks that the protection be a facility defined by the memory model of the language or library. HW.11 (and STND.CXX.23) also admit the ordering model of the hardware contract, such as interrupt masking, and confinement or immutability need no facility. Suggested fix: add "or the ordering model of the hardware contract (HW.4)" and except confinement and immutability.
