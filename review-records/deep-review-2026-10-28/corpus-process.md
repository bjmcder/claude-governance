## 1. Scope and method

I read these reading copies in full: STND, STND.CREW, STND.WFLOW, STND.WPROC, STND.DRAFT, STND.IDENT, STND.DOCS, STND.HARN, STND.ANNEX (Notes and adoption provisions; the contract tables were skimmed), STND.KNOW, STND.TEST, and STND.VVUQ. I searched the repository and its git history for the research notes that the standards cite. I weighted findings by their effect on CSQ3 and CSQ4 work, and on the promotion path, as the updated brief asks.

## 3. Systemic observations

**Onsets are hard to read.** STND.WFLOW, STND.TEST, STND.WPROC, STND.CREW, and STND.ANNEX state, sentence by sentence and in informative Notes, the class from which each part applies. STND.VVUQ states none. An agent at CSQ3 cannot see what binds it without redoing the STND.CLASS.10 analysis (CP5, CP12). A normative "From" field per provision, or one onset table per standard, would make the burden at CSQ3 and CSQ4 visible. It would also show that most methods standards treat CSQ3 the same as CSQ1 (CP4 to CP6).

**The promotion path is missing.** The corpus has two pieces with nothing between them: GOV.13 (class from intended uses) and GOV.14 (nothing grandfathered). No expected class, no list of the records that have to be made at the time, and no rule for what a reclassification owes (CP1 to CP3). The corpus also uses "promotion" in a sense other than the custodian's (CP19).

**Few human round trips at the workflow level.** Below the release, STND.WPROC and STND.CREW keep the owner out (STND.WPROC.11, the batched queue of STND.CREW.2). The remaining round trips enter through undefined identity terms (CP9, CP10), AGENT.39 (CP8), QA.5 reports (CP11), and session breaks (CP14), not through these standards' own provisions.

**The harness specification has a wide reach.** STND.HARN binds corpus layout, ledger schema, and rehearsal outcomes as conformance (CP7, CP18). Its outcome-based core (GOV.35 to GOV.39) is sound. The provisions that name "a harness" or "the checks" should be restated as outcomes that a reader is able to verify.

**The Notes are out of proportion.** The STND.WFLOW Notes alone run to about 7,000 words of source digests for 40 defaults, with drafting narration ("The custodian's order of standards of 2026-10-24 …"). The custodian's documentation direction applies to them as well.
