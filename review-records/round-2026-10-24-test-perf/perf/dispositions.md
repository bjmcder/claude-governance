# Dispositions: STND.PERF 0.1 to 0.2

Reviews of draft 0.1: review-cold.md (cold review) and review-pairs.md (STND.HARN.28 pair review). The reading copies are labeled revision 1.0 because they are composed as the standard would be adopted; the drafts are 0.1 and 0.2. Numbers are unchanged from 0.1. Only one title changed: STND.PERF.5 is now "Conditions of a relative comparison". Draft 0.2 is draft-0.2.md, its pairs are in pairs-0.2.md (590), and the 13 pairs that 0.1 did not list are in pairs-0.2-delta.md.

## Cold review

| Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| D1: STND.PERF.12 s2 and STND.PERF.14 s2 restate STND.OFFLD.31 | Accepted as suggested. Each sentence now opens "Other than for offloaded work, which STND.OFFLD.31 holds," and lists STND.OFFLD.31 in `refers_to`. STND.PERF.14 s2 now says "in a performance evaluation". The Notes say that STND.OFFLD.31 is the offload form of both sentences, that one record meets both standards, and that a later amendment of STND.OFFLD could have STND.OFFLD.31 cite them. |
| D2: STND.PERF.7, the number three has no source | Accepted. The Notes say the number rests on inference and is unverified, since p1 and p2 give no count. They name STND.CONC.24 as the analogy, for a different purpose, and mark the claim about extrapolation as p2's own synthesis. The number goes to the custodian as a decision, with a form that has no number ("enough values to distinguish the rates of growth in question"). S1 now has a way out: "or state why its variable admits fewer". |
| D3: STND.PERF.22 s1 restates RSRC.2 and RSRC.3; its onset | Accepted. S1 is cut. The provision keeps only the record of each point of the history: the revision, the HW.3 record of the path, the factors of STND.PERF.13, and the design. The onset is argued in the Notes. Each point records inputs under REPRO.1 within the tracked record of RSRC.2, and neither rests on a row. The point asks for no provision of REPRO.4 at production and no record under RSRC.3 with each run, and those parts stay with their rows at CSQ2. STND.PERF.22 therefore applies from CSQ3. |
| S4: "systematic" is circular | Accepted. The Term Performance evaluation now defines systematic checkably: the claim, the practical threshold, and the questions are stated before the measurements, and the method and the measurement design are recorded. The Notes state the reading of STND.PERF.3 and the reason for rejecting the stricter reading, that all three questions are always required, because that would block work where one question has no meaning. The reading goes to the custodian as a decision. |
| S5: STND.PERF.24 s2 against STND.VVUQ.17 is unargued | Accepted. The decision now has to keep the guard's criterion in the record (VV.3 added). The Notes give the difference and its reason: a guard on time also fails from noise, so a failure may be a false alarm, and acceptance needs evidence that each requirement is still met. |
| S6: no home for the practical threshold | Accepted. STND.PERF.3 now has each evaluation state its practical threshold with its claim. STND.PERF.19 s2 judges a difference against "the practical threshold that it stated before its measurements". |
| S7: no way out where a means is unavailable | Accepted for each. STND.PERF.23: while the history is too short to show its variation, the guard judges against the requirement and says so. STND.PERF.13: "or that the evaluation is unable to read them". STND.PERF.14 s1: "or state the error that they add"; the Notes point to timing a block of executions (STND.PERF.15). STND.PERF.21: a new sentence says MAY repeat where the record states a cause outside the code that is removed. STND.PERF.24 s1: "or state why it was not identified". STND.PERF.7 s1: as in D2. |
| S8: partial restatement | Accepted. STND.PERF.1 is one sentence: the claim, written grammatically, rests on a performance evaluation. The Notes say what it adds to RSRC.2 and RSRC.4. STND.PERF.2 drops the quantity and the size, which RSRC.1 and Budget hold. The Notes call STND.PERF.4 s2 the absolute-question form of HW.22 s3 and STND.PERF.25 RSRC.11 applied. |
| S9: method prescribed in three provisions | Accepted. STND.PERF.16 s2 states only the outcome, and the two checks move to the Notes. STND.PERF.24 s1 asks for the revision "identified by measurement", and the Notes give bisection as one way. STND.PERF.19 s1 asks for "its uncertainty and the basis of that uncertainty". |
| S10: Notes carry obligations in the indicative; length | Accepted. The sentences named are recast as reasons tied to provisions. The list of means becomes a worked example of a statistic that misfits its unit. The sentence "whoever states the requirement makes the statement" is cut. The Notes are cut from about 3,500 to about 2,800 words. |
| S11: "probes add work" unmarked | Accepted: the Notes say it follows from the layout sensitivity of p1, and that its size is unverified. |
| Optional: STND.PERF.7 s1 and s2 overlap; RSRC.5 | Accepted: S2 opens "Where a scaling claim covers values beyond those measured", and RSRC.5 is dropped. |
| Optional: STND.PERF.8 presumes a suspected resource | Accepted: "for each resource suspected of limiting the code". |
| Optional: STND.PERF.16 s2 onset (reference oracle) | Resolved by S9. The Notes say that a check against expected values the benchmark states is not a reference oracle produced without the implementation, so the check applies from CSQ3. |
| Optional: "represents"; "is one method" beside Holm | Accepted: "the use whose performance it measures". The Notes say "STND.VVUQ.35 uses the Holm step-down procedure for statistical acceptance criteria". |
| Optional: memory and energy statements unmarked | Accepted: the Notes say these statements extend p1 and p2 by inference. |
| Optional: "ratified by the custodian" in the rationale | Kept, on the lead's direction. Every rationale of the corpus is written so and applied only after ratification. |
| The drafter's decisions: MEAS reading is p2's | Accepted: the Notes say that p2 gives the reading as its own. |

## Pair review

No conflict among the 596 pairs of 0.1.

| Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| S1: STND.PERF.22 drops the case without a budget (U1) | Resolved by cold D3. S1 is cut, so the history of code with no budget is the tracked record that RSRC.2 already asks for from the first run. STND.PERF.22 asks only for what each point of that record carries, whatever quantities it tracks. |
| S2: what licenses optimization against a ratio or derived bound (U2) | Accepted in substance. The Term Performance requirement now states a ratio bound at a stated problem size. The Notes say that a ratio bound becomes a limit on a resource once its comparison base is measured. They also say that the fraction of a derived bound is not a budget, so a claim of shortfall and an optimization still need the budget or tracked record of RSRC.2 and RSRC.4. The sentence goes in the Notes and not in STND.PERF.4, because it is an explanation (STND.DRAFT.12). |
| S3: comparisons across paths outside a reported result (U3) | Accepted. STND.PERF.5 s2: where the comparison base runs on another path, the comparison meets the conditions of HW.22 whether or not its claim is reported (RSRC.4, RSRC.6; HW.22 in `refers_to`). The absolute values of the comparison base move into STND.PERF.19 s1, which keeps STND.PERF.5 at two sentences. |
| S4: STND.PERF.26 rests on an unstated reading of AGENT.40 (U4) | Accepted in part. STND.PERF.26 s1 now has the unit "hold them for the duration of its measurements". The Notes say a held resource is not left free in the sense of AGENT.40. They also say the plan of the parallel work sequences a unit that needs the machine alone, so that a stream of smaller units does not keep it waiting. Other resources, such as a shared cache or link, are left to STND.PERF.13. AGENT.22 is not cited: it records decisions that alter a plan and does not hold resources. |
| S5: STND.PERF.12 and STND.PERF.14 restate STND.OFFLD.31 (R5) | Resolved by cold D1. The citation of the STND.PERF provisions by STND.OFFLD.31 goes to the custodian as a later amendment. |
| S6: the Term Benchmark makes "versioned" definitional | Accepted as suggested: "A program, with its inputs and problem sizes, whose runs a performance evaluation measures; REPRO.10 holds that it is versioned." |
| O1: STND.PERF.18 and MEAS.3, MEAS.7; weak alternative | Accepted. The Notes say that one record meets both where MEAS binds. The alternative is now "be recorded as a decision under VV.3, with its criterion and the time at which that criterion was set". |
| O2: STND.PERF.2 and HW.20 | Accepted. STND.PERF.2 opens "Other than for a budget of a constrained target, which HW.20 holds" (HW.20 in `refers_to`). The Notes say that a deadline governed by HW.20 needs a bound on worst-case execution time, which no sample statistic gives. |
| O3: STND.PERF.22 and HW.3 | Accepted: "the record that HW.3 gives of its execution path" (HW.3 in `refers_to`). |
| O4: cite PRIME.2 in STND.PERF.21 | Not taken. Citing PRIME.2 would make the provision binding, and the custodian decided that every provision of STND.PERF is a default. |
| O5: samples across a fallback or throttle (U5) | Accepted: the Notes say that such an event enters the exclusion criterion of STND.PERF.18 or the record of the sample. |
| Smaller: STND.PERF.12 s2 and HW.22 data movement | Accepted: the Notes say that, for a claim governed by HW.22, data movement is counted whatever a measurement states of first use. |
| Smaller: STND.PERF.13 and REPRO.2 | Accepted: the Notes say that REPRO.2 still records every factor that affects results. |
| Smaller: STND.PERF.24 and STND.VVUQ.17 called counterparts | Resolved by cold S5: the Notes state the difference. |
| Smaller: STND.PERF.14 reaches AGENT.24 | Accepted: both sentences say "in a performance evaluation". |
