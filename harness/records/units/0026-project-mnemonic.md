# Unit 0026: a project's own mnemonic, registered in the ledger, in v.10

Ruling R6 of the cleanup audit of 2026-10-31, entered as revision 1.6 of STND.IDENT (ledger entries 325 to 343), has the custodians register each mnemonic other than that of a project, and has the human owner of a project register the project's mnemonic, one that the mnemonic register does not hold, by a transaction of the project's ledger (STND.IDENT.23). The Notes of STND.IDENT, section Representation in the harness, fix that transaction: "of type register, in the project's ledger, with the mnemonic as its one target and the words "project mnemonic" in its rationale, so that a harness given that ledger reads it". Finding X7 of `review-records/audit-2026-10-31/reconciliation.md` found that v.10 still asks MNEM for the first mnemonic of every identifier, so a corpus copy that holds a project constitution `<NAME>/`, copied from `_PROJECT/NAME/` as the template directs, fails v.10 unless the custodians register NAME, which STND.IDENT.23 no longer has them do. This unit makes v.10 read the registration. MNEM stays the authority for every other mnemonic, and v.11 is unchanged. The corpus `harness/governance/` is unchanged, and the verdict of `verify` on it is unchanged: every check PASS, with the one g.18 warning.

## Definition of done

1. v.10 takes as registered the first mnemonic of the items of a LVL3 project constitution (an entry of kind `project` at LVL3, and the items under it) where a transaction of the ledger the check is given, of type `register`, not scoped to MNEM, with the words "project mnemonic" in its rationale, names that mnemonic as its one target. The mnemonics after it are read under the parent `project`, as before.
2. v.10 fails on such a transaction that names other than one target in the form of a mnemonic, or a mnemonic that MNEM holds under any parent, and does not count it; the project's items then fail as unregistered, as before.
3. A mnemonic that the ledger registers counts for nothing but a LVL3 project constitution; every other identifier is read against MNEM alone. A register transaction scoped to MNEM is a change of the register, governed by v.11 as before, and registers no project mnemonic.
4. The ledger read is the one the check is given; in `verify` and `check` that is the ledger of the corpus copy. The choice is explained below.
5. Tests: a corpus copy with a project directory and no registration fails v.10; with the registration it passes; a registration of a mnemonic that MNEM holds, a malformed one, and one without the words still fail; each part of the change reverted alone fails a test.
6. The v.10 sentence of "What the logic checks cover" in `harness/README.md` says what v.10 accepts and from which ledger.

## Which ledger

STND.IDENT.23 names "the project's ledger" and the Notes add "so that a harness given that ledger reads it"; neither says where that ledger lies. Three texts settle it for a project that keeps its constitution in its corpus copy, which is where the template puts it ("Copy `_PROJECT/NAME/` to `<NAME>/`", in the corpus root). GOV.36 has the current state of governance derivable "from the corpus and the ledger alone", and the project constitution is part of that state. The adoption of the constitution (GOV.41) is a transaction whose scope pins the directory `<NAME>/` (STND.HARN.7: "corpus directories and ".md" files whose content it pins"), and the ledger check of `verify` resolves a scope against the corpus it reads, so that adoption lies in the ledger of the corpus copy, `ledger.jsonl` at its root; the registration that STND.IDENT.23 puts before the project's first instrument lies in the same chain. And STND.HARN.6 and STND.HARN.7 reach each ledger that a harness is asked to read, which `verify` and `check` are asked to read for the corpus ledger, so the form that the Notes fix is read there. The project's ledger in `verify` is therefore the ledger of the corpus copy, the one the check already reads; the unit adds no option and reads no other file. A work ledger kept under STND.DFLT.5 (`records/ledger.jsonl`) is the ledger of work without a constitution (STND.DFLT.1), and `verify` is not given it, so a project whose constitution's section Records names a ledger outside the corpus copy has its registration read only where that ledger is the one the check is given; a `--ledger-file` option of `verify` would be a separate unit, if the custodian wants one.

## Record

- 1 to 3, in `checks/revisions/identifiers.py`: `project_registrations(ledger, register)` returns the mnemonics registered and the problem lines. It reads each transaction of type `register` whose scope does not name MNEM and whose rationale holds `PROJECT_WORDS`, "project mnemonic"; a target list of other than one item, or an item that `MNEMONIC_RE` does not match (STND.IDENT.17), is a problem, and so is a mnemonic that any row of MNEM holds, whatever its parent, since STND.IDENT.23 reads "one that the mnemonic register does not hold". `_constitutions(corpus)` returns the identifiers of the entries of kind `project` at LVL3. `register_usage` (v.10) intersects the two and passes the result to `usage_problems` as `registered`; there a first segment that has no row under `none` and is in `registered` stands as registered, and the next segment is read under `project`. With no ledger, nothing is registered, and v.10 is as before. `_projects`, which v.9 reads for the forms, and `register_changes` (v.11) are unchanged. The actor and the role of the transaction are not read: the Notes fix neither, and under the defaults an agent enters the act of the human owner (STND.DFLT.17), so the actor is the agent. Nor is the order of the registration before the project's first instrument checked, nor the choice of STND.IDENT.18 against the letter distance of the project's mnemonic to the rows of MNEM; the near siblings that v.10 lists are still those of MNEM. The module docstring says what v.10 now reads.
- The template `_PROJECT/NAME/` lies in a directory whose name starts with `_`, which the corpus reader skips (`in_template`), and the live ledger holds no register transaction outside the scope MNEM, so nothing is registered on the live corpus and v.10 reads it as before.
- 5, in `tests/checks/test_revisions.py`, class `ProjectMnemonic`. `test_registration_in_the_ledger` copies the live corpus to a temporary directory, copies `_PROJECT/NAME/` to `ACME/` with each `NAME` replaced, and runs the v family on the copy and its ledger: v.10 fails with `ACME` and `ACME.WAIV.1` "not registered under `none`", and v.11 passes; after `append` of a register transaction (actor The Human Owner, scope empty, target ACME, "project mnemonic" in the rationale) v.10 and v.11 pass with no detail line; with the level of `ACME/_doc.md` set to LVL2 v.10 fails again. A project `COMP/` registered as COMP, a facet that MNEM holds under TAG, fails with "project mnemonic `COMP` is one that MNEM holds" and COMP unregistered. The targets `["ACME", "ACMEX"]`, `["Acme"]`, and `[]` each fail with the malformed line and ACME unregistered. A register transaction naming ACME without the words leaves ACME unregistered. `test_register_change_is_no_project_registration`: a custodians' register transaction scoped to MNEM, whose rationale holds the words, registers nothing and is no problem; the same transaction with an empty scope and the target ACME registers ACME.
- On a corpus copy with `ACME/` and no registration, `verify` reports logic FAIL with v.10 alone; with the registration, logic PASS with the one g.18 warning. The copy also fails the manifests and the unpinned check, since `ACME/` has no manifest and no adoption; a project writes its manifests and enters its adoption, which this unit does not touch.
- 6, `harness/README.md`, the v.10 clause of the row v.9 to v.11, and nothing else of that file. The Checks section of STND.IDENT, which describes v.10 without the project's mnemonic, and the template's sentence "NAME is the project's registered mnemonic", which X7 proposes to complete, are corpus text and are left to the custodians.
