# Unit 0010: unsigned authorizations

The security verification of unit 0008 (`review-records/round-2026-10-24/harn-auth/verify-security.md`, M-c) found that a signature is required for an authorization only where the work is assured or the corpus holds a signer register. Without a register, an agent is able to append to the ledger an unsigned `authorize` that names the human owner as actor, and to recompute the chain, since appends are permitted writes; `environment --authorization ID` then accepts it, and the printed summary is the only control. The custodian directed on 2026-10-24 that this be closed now, as a small unit. This unit refuses the derivation in that case unless the owner states, by an option, that it accepts the authorizations unsigned. The other residual of M-c, a default expiry horizon (M-b of `OPEN-ITEMS.md`), is not in scope.

## Definition of done

1. `environment` takes `--accept-unsigned`. Where `--authorization` names an authorization, the work is not assured, and the corpus holds no signer register, the derivation without the option exits 1, writes nothing, and prints the reason, beside every other reason of STND.HARN.29; it raises no uncaught exception.
2. There, with the option, the derivation proceeds as before, and the summary prints, for each authorization relied on, a line that it was accepted unsigned.
3. Where the work is assured or a signer register exists, a signature that the register verifies is required as before; the option has no effect, and the reason of a refusal says so.
4. Each criterion has a test; every earlier test passes; ruff at 79 columns adds no finding on the changed files.
5. The README and decision 0002 record the change, and this record states whether STND.HARN.29 needs an amendment.

### STND.HARN.29, revised proposal for the custodian

The decision above stands: the code conforms because it is stricter and fails closed, and the provision should still state the rule. Following the review, the statement is the derivation's and not a test of the authorization, so it moves out of the table into the body of the provision. The row Signature takes only the M1 case. A minor amendment (GOV.41), not made here:

- Row Signature, replacing its cell: "Where the work is assured, or the corpus or its ledger records a signer register, that transaction carries a signature that STND.HARN.11 accepts".
- A third sentence of the body, after the two that stand: "Where the work is not assured and neither the corpus nor its ledger records a signer register, a derived configuration SHALL rely on no unsigned authorization that the human owner has not accepted unsigned at the derivation, and SHALL name each one so accepted in its coverage report (STND.HARN.19, PRIME.7)." The condition comes first and the sentence has no trailing condition. With it the provision holds four modals, not three, so g.18 has no bundle of three to review.
- Notes, after the sentence on the row Signature: "Without a signer register, an agent able to append to the ledger is able to append an unsigned authorize that names the human owner. The row Record then does not show who granted it, and the human owner's acceptance at the derivation does (PRIME.7). A register that the ledger records and the corpus lacks was removed, so its absence lifts no signature; the ledger records one where an entry carries a signature or a transaction's scope holds the register." The floor argument that closes the Notes would name the acceptance among the costs.
- Revision row: "Minor amendment (GOV.41): STND.HARN.29: a signature is also required where the ledger records a signer register; where the work is not assured and no signer register is recorded, a derived configuration relies on an unsigned authorization only once the human owner accepts it at the derivation, and names it in its coverage report; the Notes give the reasons."
- STND.DRAFT.42 applies, since STND.HARN is LVL2: such a provision asks for no decision of the human owner other than an assigned one. The acceptance is a decision of the human owner by the term of STND.DRAFT ("an adoption, authorization, approval, ruling, or other act"). My reading is that it is assigned. GUARD.10 has an authorization granted only by the human owner and recorded, and PRIME.7 bars an agent from granting one for itself. Where nothing in the record shows the grantor, the acceptance is the human owner's own statement that the recorded grant is the owner's. It is part of the grant that LVL0 reserves to the human owner, not a new decision. It is made once per derivation, within the owner's configure act (STND.DFLT.22), not per write, so the second sentence of STND.DRAFT.42 (accept a standing authorization) holds. The owner also avoids it entirely by founding a signer register. If the custodian reads it instead as a further approval that LVL0 does not assign, two alternatives meet STND.DRAFT.42. One is to require a signature always, which raises the cost of every derivation in a corpus without a register. The other is to leave the provision as adopted and keep the refusal as this harness's stricter reading.
