# Unit 0019: the harness follows the round of 2026-10-26

The round of 2026-10-26 (`review-records/round-2026-10-26/`) amended STND.HARN, STND.DRAFT, and STND.IDENT, among others, and the custodian ratified it on 2026-10-26. This unit makes the harness follow the new text, then applies the round: ledger entries 176 to 182, and the five pins of entries 183 to 187. The items are those that `draft-status.md` lists as owed after the text.

## Definition of done

1. A ledger scope names a directory or a `.md` file (STND.HARN.7): `ledger append` and `record_transaction` accept a Markdown file, and the ledger check reports a scope that names neither. The content hash lists each file once, under the first scope path that reaches it (the term Content hash).
2. The ledger check compares each pin in force: a transaction's pin ends where a later transaction's scope reaches each of its paths (STND.HARN.9, the terms Pin in force and Reach). `append` refuses a scope that shares content with a pin in force without reaching each of its paths.
3. The check `unpinned` fails, where it warned (STND.HARN.31).
4. A new structural check `checked-by` fails on a `checked_by` key that is neither `review` nor the key of a registered check (STND.HARN.32).
5. v.5 asks for the adopter field only of a document that is adopted or ratified (STND.IDENT.12).
6. v.8 compares the provisions of each part of the constitution with the field `withdrawn` of LVL0 (STND.IDENT.24).
7. The drafting checks read the cells of defining tables, except the columns that list modals or excluded expressions (STND.DRAFT.3), or the record says why not.
8. The drafting checks end a sentence where the term Sentence does, or the record says why not.
9. `references` derives a row for a document that the text names by its identifier alone, with the exclusions of the term Cited document, and g.25 asks for no citation behind the name (decision 1, as clarified); a citation of a withdrawn identifier counts for the document that withdrew it.
10. The round is applied, the five paths are pinned, and `verify`, `references --check`, and `compose --check` pass on the live corpus.
11. Each change has a test; every test passes; ruff adds no finding; the README, OPEN-ITEMS, and the reading copies describe the change.
