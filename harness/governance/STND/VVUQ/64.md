Tests of stochastic results SHOULD be of two kinds: fixed-seed tests that detect change, and tests with fresh, logged seeds that judge correctness under the criteria of STND.VVUQ.33, STND.VVUQ.34, and STND.VVUQ.35 at each class from which those apply, and under the criterion that the acceptance criterion of the test states at every other class (NUMER.25, VV.1, VV.3).
