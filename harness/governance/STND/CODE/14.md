Where code detects a condition that PRIME.4 lists, it SHALL halt with a diagnosis, enter the designed safe state of PRIME.13 of the hardware that it commands, degrade visibly with a diagnosis, or pass the condition to its caller, in its result, in an argument that the caller passes to receive it, or by a mechanism of its language, an exception among them, that propagates the condition to the caller (PRIME.4). A record that library code writes to a log SHALL NOT be the only visible degradation (PRIME.4).
