The performance samples of each performance evaluation SHOULD be kept, as measured, with its summary (REPRO.6). Each exclusion of performance samples, runs, or benchmarks from a summary SHOULD follow a criterion recorded before the summary was seen, or be recorded, with its criterion and when it was set, as a criterion set after the summary was seen (VV.3, QA.12).
