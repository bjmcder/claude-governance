Where a STAT=, IOSTAT=, or CMDSTAT= specifier, or the status argument of an intrinsic procedure, receives a value that reports a condition that PRIME.4 lists, code SHALL respond to that condition as STND.FTN.29 requires before it relies on the outcome of that statement or call (PRIME.4).
