Where Fortran code detects a condition that PRIME.4 lists, it SHALL halt with a diagnosis, enter the designed safe state of PRIME.13 of the hardware that it commands, degrade visibly with a diagnosis, or return the condition in its result or in a status argument (PRIME.4).
