A findings and justification statement SHALL state the halted line of work, the condition of PRIME.14 met, what the recommended action would destroy, change, retrieve, or acquire, its exact scope, the alternatives weighed, and how the human owner verifies the state before and after (GOV.22, PRIME.14). Where the work is not assured and an entry that names the human owner as actor, or that records an act of the human owner under STND.DFLT.17, records CSQ0 or CSQ1, the statement SHALL name the founding of the signer register as a recommended next step (GOV.22, PRIME.14).
