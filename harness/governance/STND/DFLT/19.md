Where the work comes to hold irreplaceable data, to drive physical hardware, or to write to a system outside the provisioned environment, and the table of STND.DFLT.18 does not name that asset with the threat that the work brings to it, an agent SHALL halt that line of work and refer it (GOV.22, GUARD.16).
