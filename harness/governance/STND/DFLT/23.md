Where the work is assured, only an entry that names the human owner as actor and carries the human owner's signature, which a verifier that the agent is unable to alter confirms by a key that the signer register lists for that owner and ledger, SHALL be accepted (GOV.22, GOV.36). Where the work is not assured, each entry that names the human owner as actor or records an act of the human owner under STND.DFLT.17 SHALL be accepted (GOV.22, GOV.36).
