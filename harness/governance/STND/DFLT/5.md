Each transaction that GOV.36 lists SHALL be appended to "records/ledger.jsonl" as an entry stating its actor, role, authority, reason, and the SHA-256 hash of the previous entry (GOV.22, GOV.36). An agent SHALL NOT append an entry that names the human owner as actor (GOV.22, PRIME.7, PRIME.2).
