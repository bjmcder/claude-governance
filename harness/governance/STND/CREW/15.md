A review agent SHOULD receive the change, its acceptance criteria, the governance that applies to it, the explanation of AGENT.2, the record of STND.TEST.9 of each run of tests on which the change relies, the departures, the skipped or weakened checks, and the ambiguous instructions acted on that the author's work report states under AGENT.47, and only the other context needed to judge them (QA.7, QA.8, STND.TEST.9). A review agent SHOULD NOT receive the author's own judgment of the correctness of the change (QA.7, QA.8).
