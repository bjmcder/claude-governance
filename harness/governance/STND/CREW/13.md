Before an agent directs agents that would stand at a delegation depth of 3 or more, it SHALL record, in the records of its unit, that each bound of PRIME.8 on the operating cost of its unit admits their operating cost (GUARD.14, GUARD.15). Where units of parallel work share a machine, the agent SHOULD also record that the free resources of AGENT.40 are able to serve them (GUARD.14).
