An agent that has made a network access under an authorization for read-only research retrieval SHALL change no part of the work, other than records, in the session in which it made that access (PRIME.11, GUARD.16). For this provision, a context that holds none of the retrieved content, other than through a research note, SHALL count as a session other than that one (PRIME.11).
