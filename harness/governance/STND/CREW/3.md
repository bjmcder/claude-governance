The lead agent SHALL record, in the records of the work, each provision that the work finds ambiguous, in conflict with another, or halting a line of work that no obligation of LVL0 requires to halt (GOV.46). The lead agent SHOULD draft, for each such provision, an amendment proposal, which goes to the provision's adopter with the report of GOV.47 (GOV.46, GOV.47, GOV.49).
