A section MAY state its content by citing each provision of an adopted LVL2 standard that it adopts as the project's own (GOV.21). A project constitution MAY adopt STND.DFLT by one citation as the content of its sections Work process, Defects, Dependencies, Working notes, Workbench, Secret store, Access descriptors, Threat model, Reserved actions, Agent instructions, and Configuration, and, for Review and release, Correction, Incidents, and Records, as the content of the items of their rows that STND.DFLT provides, and state in full only its other sections, each of these whose content departs from STND.DFLT, and the items that STND.DFLT does not provide (GOV.21, GOV.22).
