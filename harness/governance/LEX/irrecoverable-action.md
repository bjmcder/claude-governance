term: "Irrecoverable action"
group: "Safeguards (PRIME.6–PRIME.14, GUARD)"
status: "ratified"
adopted: "2026-10-08"
governed_by: ["GUARD.5", "GUARD.9"]

An action whose effect on data, history, records, or state is not undoable from a verified recoverable prior state, other than an action of a class exempt under GUARD.7.
