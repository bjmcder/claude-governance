term: "Reserved action"

governed_by: ["PRIME.6", "PRIME.13"]

An action only the human owner executes: an irrecoverable or bulk action, bulk retrieval, an explicit monetary transaction, or actuation of physical hardware that the hazard analysis identifies as hazardous or irreversible, other than commanding the designed safe state.
