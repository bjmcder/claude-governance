term: "Reserved action"
group: "Safeguards (PRIME.6–PRIME.14, GUARD)"
status: "ratified"
adopted: "2026-10-08"
governed_by: ["PRIME.6"]
---

An action only the human owner executes: an irrecoverable or bulk action, bulk retrieval, an explicit monetary transaction, or hazardous or irreversible actuation of physical hardware.
