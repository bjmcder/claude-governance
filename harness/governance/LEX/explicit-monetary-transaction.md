term: "Explicit monetary transaction"
group: "Safeguards (PRIME.6–PRIME.14, GUARD)"
status: "ratified"
adopted: "2026-10-08"
governed_by: ["PRIME.6"]
---

An action whose purpose is to pay, purchase, or subscribe, or to create or raise a billing commitment or spending limit. Consumption of capacity under an existing commitment is implicit expenditure.
