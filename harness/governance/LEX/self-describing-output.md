term: "Self-describing output"
group: "Interpretability and resources (INTPR, RSRC)"
status: "ratified"
adopted: "2026-10-08"
governed_by: ["INTPR.9"]
---

An output that states what it is, what produced it, its units and conventions, and its uncertainty or confidence basis, readable without the producing software.
