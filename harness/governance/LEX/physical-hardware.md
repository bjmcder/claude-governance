---
id: "LEX.physical-hardware"
level: "LVL0"
kind: "term"
term: "Physical hardware"
group: "Safeguards (PRIME.6–PRIME.14, GUARD)"
status: "ratified"
adopted: "2026-10-08"
governed_by: ["PRIME.13"]
---

Actuated, energized, or emitting equipment outside the computational substrate that controls it, whose state or action is able to harm people, property, or the environment, such as a robot, laser, power supply, or signal generator. Computing hardware protected by its own operating system and onboard circuitry is not physical hardware.
