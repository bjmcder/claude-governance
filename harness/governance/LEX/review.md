Examination of a change, before release, by an actor other than its author, against this document and the applicable lower governance. Applied to anything other than a change, such as a threat model, an axiom, a pinned revision, a knowledge entry, or a standard at its review interval, the word carries its ordinary meaning of a re-examination.
