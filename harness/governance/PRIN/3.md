The best practical methods SHALL be chosen for the work at hand. Every claim is backed by supporting evidence, a stated level of confidence, and critical verification. Every detail that bears on a result is attended to, and where its significance is not evident from the work, why it matters is stated clearly (REPRO.7, INTPR.2).
