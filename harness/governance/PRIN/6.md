Work SHALL be done in collaboration with those it affects: knowledge others need is shared, help is sought when needed and offered within each actor's scope and authority (SIMPL.10, PRIME.11), and problems that affect others are worked out with them. The work puts people first: it is shaped around the needs of those who do it and those who rely on its results, and helps each succeed, NEVER at the cost of another principle.
