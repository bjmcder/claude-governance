## LVL0 at a glance
- 186 provisions: PRIN 10, PRIME 15, GUARD 18, VV 10, REPRO 14, INTPR 13, DSGN 17, SIMPL 11, RSRC 12, QA 15, GOV 51.
- Precedence (GOV.5): PRIME first; then GUARD, VV, REPRO, INTPR, DSGN, SIMPL, RSRC, QA (process last). PRIN and GOV unranked. Lower provisions rank with the LVL0 provision they instantiate; equal-rank conflicts resolve to the more restrictive (GOV.6).
- Non-waivable (GOV.45): PRIN, PRIME, GUARD, GOV, the floor (GOV.10), and a provision at every level that instantiates a PRIME.
- Levels (GOV.16): LVL0 constitution; LVL1 profiles (bind by selector); LVL2 standards (binding or default, GOV.19); LVL3 project instruments.
- Consequence classes (GOV.12–GOV.15): CSQ0 safety-critical to CSQ4 exploratory; no default class; CSQ0 for physical harm only on the owner's explicit statement.
- No second-human concurrence anywhere; that is parallel governance (Preamble, GOV.30).

**Revision scheme and identifiers (STND.IDENT 1.3.2, adopted 2026-10-09 by ledger transaction 10, minor 1.1 to 1.3 by transactions 18, 25, and 27, editorial 1.3.1 and 1.3.2 by transactions 35 and 45):** a revision number is major.minor, with an editorial third segment shown only above zero (1.0, 1.1, 1.1.1). Major: fundamental change to a prime directive or principle, or wholesale restructuring of articles. Minor: an ordinary amendment that leaves the fundamental motivation and intent as they were. Editorial: no obligation, permission, or defined meaning changes. A document is below 1.0 until first adopted. Each revision is a revision-history row and a ledger transaction; the field is `revision` everywhere; adopters and owners are named by role label (The Custodian, The Human Owner). Checks v.1 to v.8 enforce this. Identifiers take the form for their kind (STND.IDENT.15): LVL0; mnemonics joined by dots for documents; a number for provisions and knowledge entries; LEX or a tag facet and a slug for terms and tags; project, instrument kind, and number for LVL3 instruments; a number sign and a heading slug for locators. Mnemonics are two to five uppercase letters, registered by the custodians in MNEM by transactions of type register (STND.IDENT.19 to STND.IDENT.23). Checks v.9 to v.11 enforce this.

**verify** runs every check: structure, fields, registry, manifests, ledger, revisions, lineage, drafting, and logic. All pass on the corpus at entry 109.

**Logic checks** (ported from the retired analysis toolkit and re-targeted to LVL0 IDs and the per-file corpus): precedence is a strict total order (GOV.5) and front-matter ranks agree; the non-waivable set (GOV.45) matches the `waivable` flags, is disjoint from what the waiver clause (GOV.44) can relieve, and covers the amendment and waiver procedures themselves; no sentence puts one action in two incompatible authority tiers; no standing authorization covers a reserved action (PRIME.6); `cites` matches the identifiers in the text; excluded expressions are absent; Lexicon terms are complete and unique; and a SAT encoding proves the structural claims (built-in DPLL solver, z3 only if already importable; both agree). The logic tests inject defects into an in-memory copy and assert each is caught.

**Provisions below LVL0** carry `instantiates` and `refers_to` (identifiers or section locators such as `LVL0#normative-language`), a derived `rank` and `waivable` (GOV.17), a `mode` of binding or default with a `binding_reason` where GOV.19 needs one, `defines` for named tables, and `checked_by`. Checks l.1 to l.5 verify the lineage and always fail; checks g.1 to g.24 apply the drafting standard (STND.DRAFT) with a severity model: a defect in the standard itself fails; in other numbered provisions it fails once the standard is adopted and the provision is binding, and warns otherwise; findings in undivided drafts and in LVL0 form an advisory punch list and never fail. The drafting tests inject defects and assert each is caught.
